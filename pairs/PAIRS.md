# Pairs — brief: Make the AI judge laugh: one-line office joke, max 200 chars

# Pairwise rubric — decide each pair on ONE criterion (priority order)

1. **brief fit** — answers the competition's own brief/persona.
2. **first-glance impact** — reads clearly at 300px / in 2 seconds.
3. **emotional hook / surprise** — the thing a judge remembers after 50 pairs.
4. **beats the winners' pattern** — wins against the top-3 pattern, not a copy of it.
5. **craft** — technical quality, no artifacts/glitches.

Verdict format per pair: winner "A" or "B" + one short reason naming the
deciding criterion, e.g. "A - hook: the letter speaks to the judge".
